Before you write to us
If you have read this far and recognised something, write to us.

Do not write if you are looking for a fast-moving, casual community of like-minded peers. There are better options in Auckland and we will not be a good fit. Our meetings are formal. The pace is slow on purpose. You will be one of the youngest men in the room for some years.

Do not write if you are unwilling to take the rituals seriously. They are real, not theatrical, and most of what the lodge has to offer can only be received through them.

Write to us if something here matches a hunger you have had trouble naming.

Plainly
Meetings
First Wednesday of the month, March to December, 6.30pm. Dinner follows.
Instruction
Twice monthly. This is where the ritual is learned. It is not, in practice, optional.
Where
Ellerslie Masonic Centre, Auckland.
Cost
$80 on initiation. $140 a year. $25 for dinner, if you stay for it. You provide your own dark suit and regalia.
Required
Over 18. Of good character. A belief in a supreme being, however you understand it.
Dress
Dark suit. Always.
